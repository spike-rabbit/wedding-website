Welcome

Two Worlds, One Heart

When an Argentinian spark meets German devotion, something truly magical happens. Our love story is the union of two distinct cultures—blending South American warmth, passion, and joie de vivre with European precision, sincerity, and grounded romanticism.

Across borders, languages, and continents, we found a shared rhythm and built a home together in Switzerland. Now, surrounded by the stunning landscapes of Zurich and the people we cherish most, we are overjoyed to say 'I do' and dance into our forever.
